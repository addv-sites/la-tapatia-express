## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Response
The design system positions a traditional Mexican tortas ahogadas establishment into an elevated, mouthwatering, and conversion-engineered digital storefront. The emotional response is centered on visceral craving ("antojo inmediato"), authenticity, culinary pride, and effortless transaction. It removes cognitive friction so users navigate naturally from sensory discovery to placing an order directly via WhatsApp or visiting in person.

### Design Movement: Gastronomic Contemporary Mexican
A fusion of high-contrast tactile warmth and modern functional minimalism. Deep, chile-de-árbol reds and slow-roasted mustard tones evoke warmth and dopamine triggers, paired with cream stone surfaces and crisp obsidian accents. Layouts prioritize generous touch targets, bold food photography, and clear price tags over decorative clutter.

## Colors

### Chromatic Strategy & Conversion Neuro-Design
- **Primary (`#B91C1C` - Chile de Árbol / Carmín Artesanal):** Dominates high-priority conversion flows, active category chips, and key action buttons. Triggers appetite, urgency, and rich artisanal salsa notes.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Mostaza Tostada / Maíz Dorado):** Used for highlighting special combos, culinary badges, spicy-level indicators, and secondary tags. Stimulates dopamine, comfort, and warmth.
- **Tertiary (`#15803D` - Cilantro / Limón Fresco):** Provides balance for dietary indicators, successful additions, order confirmation cues, and verification badges.
- **Neutral (`#18181B` - Obsidiana / Carbón):** Used for high-contrast headlines, prices, and critical interface borders ensuring AAA legibility against cream backgrounds.
- **Crema BG (`#FDFBF7`) & Crema Surface (`#F7F3EA`):** Warm, inviting alternative to sterile whites; mimics parchment, stone molcajetes, and warm corn masa.
- **WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`):** Dedicated exclusively to the direct checkout transition button.

## Typography

### Structural Hierarchy & Pairings
- **Headlines & Prices (Epilogue):** An expressive, structural typeface with a crafted Mexican culinary weight. The bold, sculptural letterforms convey heritage, artisanal substance, and immediate visual presence for dish names and prices.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** A friendly, exceptionally clear geometric sans-serif tuned for mobile legibility, ingredient customization lists, address details, and checkout verification microcopy.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model & Grid System
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column layout on desktop (max container width 1200px) with 24px gutters; single-column fluid layout with horizontal-scroll category tracks on mobile (360px–430px) with 16px margins.
- **Mobile First Touch Zones:** Sticky ordering bars and checkout triggers sit within standard 48px to 64px thumb-reach zones at the bottom of the viewport.
- **Rhythm:** An 8pt baseline cadence ensures consistent padding across product cards, modifier drawers, and receipt breakdowns.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Warmth & Physical Layers
- **Tonal Grounding:** Layering uses `#FDFBF7` for the canvas background, rising to `#FFFFFF` for product cards and surface containers.
- **Shadow System:** Uses warm, umber-tinted ambient shadows (`rgba(50, 20, 10, 0.08)`) with generous blur radii instead of cold synthetic gray drops.
- **Sticky Elements & Modals:** Bottom floating cart drawers and WhatsApp floating CTAs utilize `0px 12px 32px rgba(185, 28, 28, 0.18)` to signify depth and command immediate user focus.
- **Ghost Outlines:** Product cards and customization selectors utilize a subtle 1px border (`#E5DECE`) to maintain definition against warm backgrounds without visual heaviness.

## Shapes

### Border Radius Logic
- **Base Level (`roundedness`: 2 / 0.5rem - 8px):** Standard inputs, quantity toggles, and notification toasts.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / 1rem - 16px):** Food dish cards, location panels, and pricing containers.
- **Drawers & Floating Modals (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem - 24px):** Product detail sheets, category chips, and cart summary drawers.
- **Pill Accents:** Category pills, spicy badges, and secondary tags use fully rounded ends for immediate tactile distinction.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary CTA ("Pedir ahora", "Agregar al pedido"):** Solid `#B91C1C` fill with white text in Epilogue bold, 48px height minimum, active scale transform (0.98), rounded-lg.
- **WhatsApp Checkout Button:** Solid `#25D366` fill with bold white typography, featuring the dedicated brand icon and clear total price summary.
- **Secondary Actions ("Ver menú", "Cómo llegar"):** Ghost style with 1.5px border in `#18181B` or `#B91C1C`, no shadow, providing crisp hierarchy.

### Food Product Cards
- Stacked mobile format and split-row desktop grid.
- Crisp gastronomic photography container with aspect ratio 4:3 or 1:1, followed by category tag, item name in Epilogue, brief ingredients, price badge in bold high contrast, and a direct `+` quick-add button.
- Pending price validation notice displayed via a discreet muted badge (`PRECIO REQUIERE VALIDACIÓN`).

### Modifier & Customization Drawer
- Appears as a bottom sheet on mobile and a focused modal on desktop.
- Houses salsa choices (Ahogada, Media, Separada), extra toppings (Aguacate, Queso panela, Cebolla desflemada) with explicit +/- quantity steppers, and special instruction fields.

### Cart & Direct WhatsApp Generator
- Sticky persistent bottom bar displaying item count, subtotal, and direct order trigger.
- Expanded state renders a clean list breakdown with itemized extras, customer name input, pickup/delivery note selector, and the pre-formatted WhatsApp output preview.
